What did the flights show?

With tilt lead on, both stacks' shortest confirmed lap is 33 seconds.

Both failed at 32 s. CogniPilot came closer: its largest error at 32 s was 0.742 to 0.757 m, against Betaflight's single flight at 0.771 m. Moving the 0.75 m limit by a few millimetres would change the result, which is why the limit is fixed.
