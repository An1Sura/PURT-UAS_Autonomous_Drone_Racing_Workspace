Can we finish in time, and what could go wrong?

The computing work fits in the week, if we follow an ordered plan.

The larger risks are the unknown state of the virtual machine, stalled flights, and the time needed to analyse and publish. The first step is to write down the rule before reading any new results.

What happens each day?

  1. Monday night to Tuesday morning: commit the rule. Copy everything off the virtual machine. Measure how often flights stall.
  2. Tuesday: fly Betaflight with lead off. Rerun Betaflight at 32 s and 33 s on the same runner. Calculate the lag from the 40 s pair. Decide whether CogniPilot flights are working.
  3. Tuesday night to Thursday: fly CogniPilot with lead off, then lead on. Run 15 confirmations at the shortest lap time that passes.
  4. Friday: stop new flights at noon. Score the results.
  5. Saturday and Sunday: update the documents and the replay. Check the published pages. Prepare the slides.
