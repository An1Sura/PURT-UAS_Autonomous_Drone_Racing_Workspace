Is CogniPilot faster than Betaflight?

With tilt lead on, no. The two stacks tie at 33 seconds.

Both pass 33 s on all 15 runs, and both fail at 32 s. The tilt-lead-off tests, which could separate them, have not been flown yet.

What do we know so far?

Betaflight, lead on

33 s

All 15 runs met the tracking limits. 13 of 15 landed. The one flight at 32 s missed the 0.75 m limit, at 0.771 m.

CogniPilot, lead on

33 s

All 15 runs met the tracking limits and all 15 landed. At 32 s, 2 of 15 passed. Most of the others missed by 2 to 7 mm.

Verdict under the rule

Tie

Indistinguishable at 1 s resolution. CogniPilot's largest error was 8 to 25 mm lower than Betaflight's in all 11 distinct matched flights. That is a measured margin, not a faster lap.

What could change the answer?

Tilt lead off. At 33 s, CogniPilot's five lead-off flights had a largest error of 0.48 m, against 0.71 m with lead on. That leaves room for shorter laps. Nobody has searched for them yet. Betaflight has never flown lead off on this test bed.

What does "faster" mean here?

It means the shortest planned lap that a drone's flight software can follow while staying within limits. It is not a race time, and it is not how fast a computer runs.

Both stacks fly the same 47-metre figure-eight in a computer simulation. A lap time counts only if all 15 test runs meet every limit:

  • The typical error stays below 0.35 m.
  • The largest error stays below 0.75 m.
  • The drone never reaches its tilt limit.
  • The drone covers the whole course in the right order.

Each stack uses the better of two settings: tilt lead on or tilt lead off. Equal times are a tie. We report landing separately. The full rule is in docs/results/arm-speed/speed-rule.json. It was written after the lead-on results were known, so only the lead-off tests are scored blind.

Does landing change the answer?

Choose a way of scoring. The results are for tilt lead on.

Be careful with the landing result. In one matched test, Betaflight's landing failure went away when only its motor protocol changed. With the PWM protocol we chose, its control loop ran at 500 times a second. With OneShot125 it ran at 2000 times a second and landed. The landing difference may come from our setup, not from Betaflight.

What are the limits of these results?

  • Every result comes from a simulation. None of it describes real hardware timing.
  • "Betaflight" here is a proposed port to this chip, running with a simulation adapter.
  • The noise levels are assumed. They are not measured from a real drone.
  • Results apply only to the lap times we tested.
  • This is an exploratory result, not a qualified ranking. Gain retesting on this chip and some command checks are still open.

What do these words mean?

Stack

The flight software on the drone. Here, the stacks are CogniPilot and Betaflight.

Typical error (RMS)

A kind of average of how far the drone was from where it should have been at each moment. RMS stands for root mean square.

Largest error

The farthest the drone got from where it should have been at any one moment. A drone that is on the path but late still counts as being in error.

Tilt lead

An extra tilt command that tries to start a turn slightly early. It can be on or off.

Gain K

A setting for how strongly the drone corrects its tilt. Both stacks use K = 6 per second.

Confirmation

A set of 15 runs at one lap time, using three noise levels and five random seeds. The five no-noise runs give the same result, so there are 11 distinct flights.

Motor protocol

How the flight software sends commands to the motors. In this simulation, Betaflight's choice of protocol also set how often its control loop ran.

How did we write these pages?

We wrote these pages using the four principles of plain language in ISO 24495-1:2023:

  • Relevant: each page starts with the answer readers came for.
  • Findable: headings are questions, and every page has the same menu.
  • Understandable: sentences are short, and technical words are explained above.
  • Usable: each chart has a table with the same data, and readers can test the numbers themselves.
