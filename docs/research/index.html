03 / CONFERENCE QUESTIONS

What we want to establish

Timing & reliability

How do loop execution time, period jitter, deadline misses and sample age compare? How much overhead comes from the Zephyr acquisition path?

NOT YET MEASURED

Report median, p95, p99, maximum, sample count, test duration and dropped samples. Separate simulated time, emulator wall time and physical-board measurements.

Estimation methods

Compare current propagation with trapezoidal / midpoint and first-order-hold candidates on identical timestamped IMU inputs.

RESEARCH PLAN

The inspected Modelica estimator already uses Lie-group propagation. New algorithms must demonstrate accuracy and execution-cost benefits; none are claimed yet.

Competitive performance

Use ArduPilot as an initial performance target and Betaflight as the higher-performance benchmark, with PX4 as an additional reference.

TARGET, NOT RESULT

Winning requires matched conditions and repeatable evidence. Current recordings do not show that CogniPilot outperforms any stack in hardware loop speed.

05 / NEXT EXPERIMENTS

From working simulation to defensible claims

  1. Requalify the NXP baseline. Same-rule attitude tuning, lead on/off, speed sweeps and repeated noise/seed cells.
  2. Instrument execution. Capture estimator/control boundaries, jitter and reliability counters; state the emulator limitations.
  3. Compare integrators. Test constant rotation, coning, acceleration and irregular sample intervals; include buffering latency and covariance behavior.
  4. Expand the matched benchmark. Add ArduPilot and PX4 with explicit hardware targets and equivalent measurement boundaries.
